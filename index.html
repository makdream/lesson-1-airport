Grammar: Present Simple + can/can't

⏱ 12 min

Present Simple — Констатация фактов и расписания

Используем для:

  • Фактов: "The plane lands at 2 PM."
  • Расписания: "The flight departs at 9 AM."
  • Привычек: "I always check-in online."
I fly to Bangkok every month.
The plane takes off at 10 AM.
The flight doesn't leave until 12 PM.
Does the airport have free Wi-Fi?

can / can't — Разрешения и запреты

We can board the plane now. — Мы можем подняться на борт.
You can't smoke here. — Нельзя курить здесь.
Can I take this bag on the plane? — Можно взять сумку?
She can check-in online. — Она может зарегистрироваться онлайн.
Present Simple can / can't
Факты, расписание, привычкиРазрешение ("can I...?")
I fly / He flies (+s/es)can + verb / can't + verb
Does it...? / It doesn't...Can I...? — вопрос

Listening: Airport Announcements

⏱ 8 min
Listen to the announcements. First time — try to understand the main idea. Second time — listen for details.

🔑 Key Phrases to Know First

is now boarding — начинается посадка

The flight has been delayed — рейс задерживается
Final call — последний призыв
We apologize for the inconvenience — приносим извинения за неудобства
📢 Announcement 1 — Boarding

"Good afternoon, passengers of flight JL 451 to Tokyo.

Boarding will begin in fifteen minutes.

Please proceed to gate A-12.

Please have your boarding pass and passport ready."

📢 Announcement 2 — Delay

"Attention, passengers of flight JL 451 to Tokyo.

The flight has been delayed.

The new departure time is 3:30 PM.

We apologize for the inconvenience.

Please stay near gate A-12."

📢 Announcement 3 — Final Call

"Final call for flight JL 451 to Tokyo.

Passengers, final boarding call.

Please go to gate A-12 immediately.

The gate will close in five minutes."

Speaking: Check-in Role-Play

⏱ 10 min
You arrive at the airport 2 hours before your flight. Practice this dialogue. Then switch roles!

✈️ Dialogue: At the Check-in Desk

👨‍✈️ Agent: Good morning! Your passport, please.
🧑 Passenger: Here you are. (Вот мой паспорт)
👨‍✈️ Agent: Do you have any checked luggage? (Сдаёте ли багаж)
🧑 Passenger: Yes, one bag. (Да, одна сумка)
👨‍✈️ Agent: Is it under 23 kg? (Вес до 23 кг?)
🧑 Passenger: I think so. (Думаю, да)
👨‍✈️ Agent: Would you like a window or an aisle seat? (Место у окна или у прохода?)
🧑 Passenger: Window seat, please. (У окна, пожалуйста)
👨‍✈️ Agent: Here is your boarding pass. Boarding starts at 9:45 at gate C-5. (Ваш посадочный талон. Посадка начинается в 9:45 у выхода C-5.)
🧑 Passenger: Can I take my carry-on bag with me? (Можно ручную кладь с собой?)
👨‍✈️ Agent: Yes, of course. Have a nice flight! (Да, конечно. Приятного полёта!)
🧑 Passenger: Thank you very much! (Спасибо большое!)

🎯 Variations — Try These Situations Too

Situation 1: Your flight is delayed. Ask the agent questions:

  • "What is the new boarding time?"
  • "How long is the delay?"
  • "Can I change my seat?"

Situation 2: Your carry-on bag is too heavy. You need to check it:

  • "Is my bag too heavy?"
  • "Can I check it instead?"
  • "How much does it cost?"

Situation 3: You arrive late. The final call is announced:

  • "Is boarding still open?"
  • "Which gate is it?"
  • "Can I go first, please?"

💡 Speaking Tips for a Traveler

  • Don't worry about mistakes — airports are high-stress places, people are understanding!
  • Always have "Excuse me, could you help me?" ready
  • If you don't understand: "Sorry, could you repeat that, please?"
  • Numbers are important — practice saying gates, times, and weights

🔑 Questions the Officer Asks

Your passport, please. — Пожалуйста, ваш паспорт.
What is the purpose of your visit? — Какова цель вашего визита?
Business or tourism? — По делам или туризм?
How long are you staying? — Как долго вы останетесь?
Where are you staying? — Где вы остановитесь?
Is this your first time in Bali? — Вы впервые на Бали?

💬 How to Answer

I'm here on business. — Я здесь по делам.
I'm here on holiday. — Я здесь в отпуске.
For two weeks. — На две недели.
At a hotel in Denpasar. — В отеле в Денпасаре.
No, it's my third time. — Нет, третий раз.

🛂 Dialogue: At Passport Control

🛂 Officer: Good morning. Welcome to Bali! Your passport, please. (Доброе утро. Добро пожаловать на Бали! Ваш паспорт, пожалуйста.)
🧑 Passenger: Here you are. (Вот он.)
🛂 Officer: What is the purpose of your visit? (Какова цель вашего визита?)
🧑 Passenger: I'm here on business. I sell swimming pools. (Я здесь по делам. Я продаю бассейны.)
🛂 Officer: Nice! How long are you staying? (Отлично! Как долго вы останетесь?)
🧑 Passenger: For ten days. (На десять дней.)
🛂 Officer: And where are you staying? (А где вы остановитесь?)
🧑 Passenger: At a hotel in Denpasar, near the beach. (В отеле в Денпасаре, недалеко от пляжа.)
🛂 Officer: Is this your first time in Bali? (Вы впервые на Бали?)
🧑 Passenger: No, it's my third time. I love it here. (Нет, третий раз. Мне здесь очень нравится.)
🛂 Officer: Have a nice stay! Here is your passport. (Приятного пребывания! Вот ваш паспорт.)
🧑 Passenger: Thank you very much! (Огромное спасибо!)

💡 Tips for Passport Control

  • Keep your passport and boarding pass at hand
  • Answer with short sentences: "Business" or "Tourism" is enough
  • If you don't understand: "Sorry, could you repeat that, please?"
  • Bali allows visa-free entry for many countries for 30 days / visa on arrival

✅ Tasks to Complete

1. Grammar: Fill the gaps

1. Passengers ________ (can / can't) use their phones during takeoff.

2. The flight to Bangkok ________ (depart) at 8 AM.

3. You ________ (can / can't) check in online — it's faster.

4. Boarding ________ (start) 30 minutes before the flight.

5. Smokers ________ (can / can't) smoke in the terminal.
